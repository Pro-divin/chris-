About Nach Promophar Ltd

Founded in 2020, Nach Promophar Ltd has rapidly established itself as a trusted partner in the healthcare and consumer goods sectors. Headquartered in Nyamata, Rwanda, with offices in Bujumbura and DRCongo, the company provides specialized marketing, promotion, and regulatory affairs services for a diverse range of products including pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, and nutritional milk powders.

Mission: To bridge the gap between innovative health products and the end-user through ethical promotion and stringent regulatory compliance.

With a dedicated team of over 12 professionals, including Nurses, Pharmacists, regulatory experts, and marketing strategists, Nach Promophar has successfully launched and managed over 100 product lines across 3 African markets, reflecting our strong market presence and client trust.

Core Services

1. Pharmaceutical & Cosmetic Marketing

We design and execute comprehensive B2C marketing tailored to the unique needs of each product category. Our team leverages both traditional and digital channels to maximize brand visibility and product adoption.

  • Brand strategy development for prescription and OTC drugs
  • Point-of-sale promotions and in-pharmacy detailing
  • Cosmetic product launches with influencer partnerships

2. Nutritional Milk Product Promotion

Our expertise extends to the dairy and infant nutrition sector, where we promote high-quality milk powders and fortified nutritional products.

  • Medical detailing for infant formula and adult nutritional milk
  • In-store sampling and consumer education programs
  • Partnerships with maternal and child health organizations

3. Regulatory Affairs

Navigating the complex regulatory landscape is our specialty. We provide end-to-end support from product registration to post-marketing surveillance.

  • Product registration and dossier preparation
  • Pharmacovigilance and adverse event reporting
  • Regulatory strategy consulting for new market entry
